# C13 owner answers: a booster quote fits the patient's age band (2026-11-23)

Tier 2, item 6 (Phase 8, C13: "A quote never says the opposite of its card, and is written about
the card's own age band"). Ported from PneumoVax FINDING N.

## What ACIP says (read live 2026-11-23, rr6909a1.htm, each table's rows)

- **Table 4 (complement) and Table 5 (asplenia):** a "2–9 yrs" row with "Aged <7 yrs: Single dose
  at 3 yrs after primary vaccination and every 5 yrs thereafter" and "Aged ≥7 yrs: Single dose at
  5 yrs …" (Table 5 words them slightly differently), and a separate **"≥10 yrs"** row: "Single
  dose at 5 yrs after primary vaccination and every 5 yrs thereafter".
- **Table 6 (HIV) and Table 9 (travel):** one "≥2 yrs" row holding both "Aged" sentences. For
  them "Aged ≥7 yrs" is their own row at every age.
- Table 8 (outbreak) prints its "Aged <7 / ≥7" sentences in its ≥10 row too: no change needed.

## What the app did (measured over 22,536 saved patients)

- 6,550 MenACWY dose 1 / dose 2 cards for patients aged 7 or older read "Boosters: 3 years after
  the series if it ends before 7 years [c], otherwise 5 [c], then every 5 years while at risk",
  quoting the "Aged <7 yrs" sentence to a patient whose series cannot end before 7.
- About 4,400 showings of the 2–9 row's "Aged ≥7 yrs" sentence to complement or asplenia patients
  whose series ended at 10 or older.

## Owner answers (2026-11-23)

1. **Part 1, "Drop the under-7 clause".** From age 7, the high-risk dose 1 / dose 2 booster line
   reads "Boosters: 5 years after the series [c], then every 5 years while at risk", quoting the
   row for the age the series will end at. Under 7 the line is unchanged (both halves, both quotes).
2. **Part 2, "Say '10 years or older'".** A complement or asplenia series that ended at 10 or older:
   "Series complete. The first booster is 5 years later because the series ended at 10 years or
   older [c]", quoting the ≥10 row. A series ended at 7–9, and HIV or travel at any age, keep
   "7 years or older".

## Decided alone (citation only, same doses: Q12)

- New quote `boosterFrom10` (Tables 4 and 5, ≥10 row).
- Every MenACWY high-risk booster citation (card booster line, series-complete note, later
  boosters, infant-series boosters, and the recorded-dose "booster too soon" verdict) quotes the
  row for the age the series ended at: under 7, 7–9, or 10+ (complement, asplenia). A later
  booster used to quote the "Aged ≥7" row whatever that age was.

No dose, date, status or brand moved.
