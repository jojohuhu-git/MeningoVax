# Owner decisions, 2026-11-21: C4, ACIP 2020 on the MenACWY exposure cards

Asked in the session that built C4 document 1, part 2 (ACIP 2020, rr6909a1).
Both are wording or source questions: no dose, date or status changes under
either answer (saved-answers sweep green).

## E1. Lab-exposure card for a child under 10 — answer: "Same as 10+"

**Question.** ACIP 2020 Table 7 (microbiologists) has only a "≥10 yrs" row.
A child under 10 with "Microbiologist routinely exposed to N. meningitidis"
checked gets the same card as at 10+, plus the yellow age-doubt note ("ACIP
lists it only for ages 10 and over"). The card's ACIP 2020 button had no
number (444 grid cards). Options: cite the same as 10+; remove the button;
leave it unnumbered.

**Source read live 2026-11-21.** ACIP's summary: "ACIP also recommends routine
vaccination with MenACWY for persons aged ≥2 months at increased risk ...
including ... microbiologists routinely exposed to isolates of Neisseria
meningitidis".

**Answer.** Same as 10+: the dose sentence cites the summary sentence and the
booster line cites Table 7's "Single dose at 5 yrs after primary vaccination
and every 5 yrs thereafter". The age-doubt note stays.

## E2. College card wording — answer: "Match ACIP"

**Question.** The college card for a resident whose only dose came before 16
said, behind "Why this": "The college-residence requirement is met only by a
dose given on or after the 16th birthday." (324 grid cards.)

**Source read live 2026-11-21** (Table 10 footnote): "The preferred timing of
the most recent dose is on or after their 16th birthday. If only 1 dose of
vaccine was administered before the 16th birthday, a booster dose should be
administered before enrollment."

**Answer.** Match ACIP. Built as two sentences (the owner's style rule 4
allows no semicolon, so the approved wording's semicolon became a full stop;
words unchanged): "ACIP prefers the most recent dose on or after the 16th
birthday [c]. A single dose before 16 needs a booster before enrollment [c]."
