## Sources the answers rest on (quoted in the packet)

- Q1: ACIP 2020, "a MenB booster dose 1 year after primary series completion, then every 2–3 years
  thereafter"; owner C9 (2026-11-15), an earlier booster does not count.
- Q2: CDC, "Clinical Guidance for Managing Meningococcal Disease Risk in Patients Receiving
  Complement Inhibitor Therapy", updated March 6, 2026, read live 2026-11-22.
- Q5: ACIP 2020 Table 8, "Single dose if ≥1 yr after MenB primary series completion (≥6 mos interval
  might also be considered by public health professionals)"; ACIP 2020 text, "interval of ≥6 months
  may be considered if recommended by public health officials".

## Correction to the packet (2026-11-22)

recommended by public health officials". A live re-read on 2026-11-22 did not find it in the
page text that could be read word for word (Box 1, Box 2 and Tables 1–3 were not read word for
word). The page's own words are in Table 8: "public health professionals". Q5 cites Table 8 only.
